##  Problem Statement

RACV is a large insurance and roadside assistance company. Members often need fast, trustworthy answers about products, services, claims, roadside assistance, and self-service tasks.

Design a **customer-facing chatbot app** that answers questions using **RACV Help & Support** content as its knowledge source. The chatbot should help users find accurate information without inventing policy, pricing, eligibility, or operational details.

---

##  Part 1: System Design

In the design portion, walk through the architecture you would use for a real customer-facing RACV chatbot.

This is a live discussion with the interviewers. We expect you to ask clarifying questions, state assumptions, and talk through your design as you develop it.

You may use markdown files or excalidraw for the discussion notes and diagrams.
